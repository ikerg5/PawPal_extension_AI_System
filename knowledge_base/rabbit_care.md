# Rabbit Care Guidance

## Feeding
Rabbits need constant access to hay, plus a scheduled check-in twice a day (morning
and evening, about 10 minutes) to refresh hay, refill water, and offer a measured
portion of pellets and fresh greens. Feeding/hay-check is a high-priority daily task.

## Enrichment
Rabbits need daily out-of-enclosure exercise time, roughly 30-60 minutes, in a
rabbit-proofed space, plus chew toys to manage constantly-growing teeth. Treat
exercise time as a high-priority daily task.

## Grooming
Weekly brushing (10-15 minutes) is enough for short-haired rabbits; long-haired
breeds need brushing every 1-2 days to prevent matting and painful hair ingestion.
Medium-priority, weekly-to-several-times-weekly task.

## Safety notes
Rabbits hide illness well; a sudden drop in eating or stool output is an emergency
sign, not a minor issue. The correct response is always to consult a veterinarian —
do not attempt to diagnose symptoms or recommend medication or dosages.
