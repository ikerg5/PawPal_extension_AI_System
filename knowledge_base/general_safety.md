# General Pet Safety Guidance (all species)

This document applies to every species and should always be considered alongside
any species-specific guidance.

## Medication and medical decisions
Never determine, suggest, or imply a medication name, dosage, or frequency for any
pet. Never attempt to diagnose a symptom or condition. Any task involving
medication administration should be scheduled generically (e.g. "Give medication as
prescribed by vet") without inventing specifics, and any owner question about
symptoms, illness, or treatment should be redirected to "consult your veterinarian."

## Scheduling sanity
Care tasks should have realistic durations (most daily care tasks fall between 5 and
90 minutes) and a priority that reflects real consequences of skipping the task:
high priority for anything affecting immediate health/safety (feeding, essential
medication reminders, litter/waste management), medium priority for wellbeing
(exercise, enrichment), and low priority for cosmetic/maintenance tasks (grooming,
brushing) unless a specific coat/skin condition changes that.
