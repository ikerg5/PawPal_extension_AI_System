# Dog Care Guidance

## Walking
Most adult dogs need 30-60 minutes of walking or active exercise per day, split into
1-2 sessions. High-energy breeds (herding, sporting) often need closer to 60-90
minutes; senior or small-breed dogs may only need 20-30 minutes. A brisk morning
walk is a common high-priority daily task since it covers both exercise and bathroom
needs.

## Feeding
Adult dogs are typically fed 2 times per day (morning and evening), about 10 minutes
per feeding including prep and cleanup. Puppies under 6 months often need 3-4
smaller feedings per day. Feeding should always be a high-priority daily task since
missing it affects health quickly.

## Grooming
Brushing 2-3 times per week (10-15 minutes) is enough for most short-to-medium coat
dogs; long-haired breeds may need daily brushing. Grooming is generally a
medium-to-low priority, weekly-frequency task unless the dog has a coat condition.

## Enrichment
Short daily play or training sessions (10-20 minutes) help prevent boredom-related
behavior issues, especially in high-energy breeds. Treat this as a medium-priority
daily task alongside the walk.

## Safety notes
Watch for signs of overheating on hot-weather walks and avoid hot pavement on paws.
For anything involving symptoms, appetite changes, or possible illness, the correct
action is always to consult a veterinarian directly — do not attempt to diagnose or
recommend medication or dosages.
