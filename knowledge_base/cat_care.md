# Cat Care Guidance

## Feeding
Adult cats are typically fed 2 times per day, about 10 minutes per feeding. Cats
free-fed dry food still benefit from a scheduled check to refill and monitor intake,
since appetite changes are an early illness signal. Feeding is a high-priority daily
task.

## Litter box
Scooping the litter box once or ideally twice a day (5-10 minutes) keeps the box
usable and lets an owner notice health changes (litter clumps, frequency). This is a
high-priority daily task — cats commonly refuse a dirty box.

## Grooming
Short-haired cats mostly self-groom and need brushing only about once a week
(10 minutes); long-haired breeds benefit from brushing every 1-2 days to prevent
matting. Treat as a low-to-medium priority task.

## Enrichment
10-20 minutes of daily interactive play (wand toys, chasing) satisfies a cat's
predatory instinct and reduces stress-related behavior problems. Medium-priority
daily task.

## Safety notes
Sudden changes in litter box habits, appetite, or energy can indicate illness. The
correct response is always to consult a veterinarian — do not attempt to diagnose
symptoms or recommend medication or dosages.
